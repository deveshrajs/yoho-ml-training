# Week 3 – Day 3: Applied Solution Architecture & Decision Framework

*(Note: ⭐ indicates the two enterprise problems most frequently misdiagnosed with generative LLMs.)*

## Enterprise Solution Decision Matrix

| # | Business Problem | Input Modality | Target Output | Key Constraints (Latency, Cost, Auditability) | Recommended Technology | Human-in-the-Loop Required? | Architectural Justification |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **Leave balance lookup** | Structured DB query (`employee_id`, `leave_type`) | Exact numerical balance (integer days) | Latency < 50ms, zero marginal cost, 100% mathematical certainty | **SQL Query** | **No** | Reading structured relational records requires exact indexing and retrieval; statistical models or LLMs introduce latency and hallucination risk. |
| **2** | **Invoice tax calculation** ⭐ | Structured numbers (`taxable_amount`, `hsn_code`, `state_code`) | Exact tax amount (decimal currency) | Latency < 10ms, zero API cost, strict statutory auditability | **Python Function / Business Rules** | **No** | *(Commonly misdiagnosed with LLM)* Statutory tax rules are 100% deterministic mathematical formulas. Probabilistic LLMs suffer from arithmetic drift and high token costs. |
| **3** | **Resume screening & candidate ranking** | Unstructured PDF/Word resumes + Job Description | Ranked candidate shortlist + relevance match score | Batch processing (seconds), high explainability, anti-bias compliance | **Embedding Similarity + Classical ML** | **Yes** | Semantic embeddings capture role alignment, but ethical standards and employment regulations mandate human recruiter review before disqualification. |
| **4** | **HR policy Q&A assistant** | Natural language queries + internal policy handbooks | Grounded explanatory answer with document citations | Latency < 3s, low-cost retrieval, zero unsupported claims | **RAG + LLM (Hybrid Search)** | **No** *(Escalates low-confidence)* | Requires semantic understanding over unstructured internal PDFs, strictly grounded via dense/sparse chunk retrieval to prevent hallucination. |
| **5** | **Employee attrition forecasting** | Historical HRMS attributes, tenure, compensation, performance | Churn probability (`0.0–1.0`) + top risk drivers (SHAP) | Weekly/monthly batch, high explainability, calibrated threshold | **Supervised ML (XGBoost / LightGBM)** | **Yes** | Identifies non-linear patterns across multi-year employee data; HR business partners must validate risk profiles before executing retention outreach. |
| **6** | **Automated customer email replies** | Unstructured incoming customer email text | Contextual draft email response | Latency < 5s, brand tone consistency, factual correctness | **RAG + LLM with Guardrails** | **Yes** | Generates fluent personalized responses, but automated outbound commitments require human agent approval to prevent customer commitments from hallucinated text. |
| **7** | **Monthly statutory payroll execution** ⭐ | Structured time-sheets, attendance logs, tax slabs, benefits | Validated payroll ledger, payslips, bank NEFT transfer file | Strict zero-error tolerance, batch run, complete audit trail | **Python Rules Engine + SQL Engine** | **Yes** | *(Commonly misdiagnosed with LLM)* Payroll calculations must adhere strictly to tax codes and labor laws; finance teams must perform final sign-off before fund disbursements. |
| **8** | **New hire onboarding provisioning** | New hire event trigger + employee role profile | Orchestrated API calls (Slack, Jira, Google Workspace, GitHub) | Asynchronous execution (1–3 min), transactional rollback | **AI Agent Orchestrator (Multi-Tool)** | **Yes** | Coordinates sequential actions across heterogeneous third-party SaaS APIs, requiring administrative approval before granting elevated permissions. |
